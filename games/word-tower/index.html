How to Play

  1. First word starts with A.
  2. Each next word starts with the previous word's last letter.
  3. Each letter is an individual brick; the whole word forms one player-colored slab.
  4. Submit before the timer you set on the start screen runs out.
  5. Words are checked online against a dictionary when internet access is available; repeated words are not allowed.
  6. Invalid word or timeout eliminates the player.
  7. Last player remaining wins.
Prototype: local turns and simple bots only; no online multiplayer yet. Online dictionary validation requires an internet connection.
