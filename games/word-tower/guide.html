Game guide

How to play Word Tower

Word Tower is a word-chain game for 2 to 8 players. Each word starts with the last letter of the word before it, and every word becomes a colorful slab in a growing tower. Think fast, because the timer is always running.

Play Word TowerAll games

The rules

  1. The first word of the game must start with the letter A.
  2. Every word after that must start with the last letter of the previous word. If the last word was TIGER, the next word starts with R.
  3. Words must be 2 to 18 letters long, using letters only.
  4. A word can only be used once in a game.
  5. You must submit a word before your turn timer runs out. If time runs out, you are out of the game.
  6. The last player remaining wins.

If you type a word that does not start with the right letter, was already used, or is not found in the dictionary, you get a message and can try another word as long as time remains.

Scoring

Each accepted word scores one point for every letter, so longer words are worth more. Points decide the order on the results screen, but the winner is always the last player standing.

Game modes

  • Pass and play: 2 to 8 players share one device and take turns.
  • Play vs bots: you play against computer players. Choose Easy, Normal, or Hard. Hard bots deliberately pick words that end in tricky letters.

Before you start, you can set the number of players, enter their names, and choose how many seconds each turn lasts, from 5 to 60.

Tips

  • Choose words that end in a difficult letter, such as X, Z, Q, or J, to leave the next player struggling.
  • Keep a few short words in mind for every letter. Letters like U, I, and O can be hard to start a word with in a hurry.
  • Do not spend your best words early. Long words score well, but a quick safe answer keeps you alive.
  • Watch the highlighted last letter on the top slab of the tower. It is the letter you need.

Questions

Why was a real word rejected?

Words are checked against a built-in list and then online dictionaries. Very new words, slang, names, and unusual forms may not be found. Try a different form of the word or another word.

What if I am offline?

If the game cannot reach the online dictionaries, it trusts your word so the game can continue.

Is my progress saved?

The tallest tower you have built is saved in your browser on that device. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Can I add it to my home screen?

Yes. Use the Install button on the start screen on Android and desktop Chrome or Edge. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
