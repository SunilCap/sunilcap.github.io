How to play Stack Up

Stack Up is a one-tap timing game. A block slides across the screen above your tower, and your job is to drop it so it lands as neatly as possible. The better you line it up, the taller your tower can grow.

Play Stack UpAll games

The basics

  1. Press Start stacking. A block begins sliding from side to side above the tower.
  2. Tap, click, or press Space to drop it.
  3. Any part of the block that hangs over the edge of the tower is sliced off and falls away. Only the part that lands on the tower is kept.
  4. Each block you keep makes the next one the same width, so the tower gets narrower every time you miss the alignment.
  5. If a block misses the tower completely, the game ends. Your score is the number of floors you stacked.

Perfect drops and combos

If a block lands almost exactly on top of the one below, it counts as a perfect drop. You keep the full width, you hear a rising note, and your combo grows. After a few perfect drops in a row, your blocks slowly grow back toward their starting width, which is the best way to survive for a long time.

Tips

  • Watch the edge of the sliding block, not its middle. Line the edges up with the tower below.
  • Find the rhythm. The block moves at a steady speed, so tapping a little early or late is something you can learn to repeat.
  • A small mistake is fine. A thin sliver being sliced off costs far less than a late tap.
  • The blocks speed up gradually as your tower grows, and the sky changes from day to sunset to night. Stay calm as it gets faster.

Questions

Is my best score saved?

Yes. Stack Up saves your best score in your browser's local storage on that device. Clearing your browser data will reset it.

Can I play it like an app?

Yes. On Android and desktop Chrome or Edge, use the Install button on the start screen. On iPhone or iPad, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen. Once the game has been opened, it can also load without a connection.

Does it cost anything?

No. Stack Up is free to play in your browser.
