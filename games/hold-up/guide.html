Game guide

How to play Hold Up

Hold Up is a one-finger climbing game. Hold to rise through gaps in a never-ending stack of bars, slide to steer, and see how many rows you can climb before the bars sweep you away.

Play Hold UpAll games

The goal

Your ball starts on the ground. Above it is a stack of bars, each with a gap. Climb through the gap in each bar to reach the next row. Your score is the number of rows you have climbed, and your best score is saved on your device.

The two kinds of bars

  • Teal bars are steady. They stay still, so they are safe places to rest while you wait for the right moment.
  • Orange bars slide. They carry the ball sideways in the direction of the arrows. Wait too long on one and you will be swept off the screen. Gaps in orange bars also move, and a gap sliding under a resting ball will let it drop to the row below.

Tips

  • Watch the gap above you before you launch. Rising takes a fraction of a second, so aim for where the gap will be.
  • Letting go makes you fall back to the bar below, so commit to a climb once you start.
  • Steering is slower than the fastest orange bars. Fight the slow ones, and leave the fast ones quickly.
  • Don't steer into the side of a bar while passing through a gap, as touching a bar while rising ends the run.
  • As you climb, more bars move, they move faster, and the gaps get narrower. The sky also changes from day to sunset to night.

Questions

Can I play offline? Yes. Once you have opened the game, you can add it to your home screen and play it like an app, including offline.

Is my score saved? Your best score is stored in your browser on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Why do ads appear? Scotcha is free to play and supported by Google ads, which may appear between rounds. If ads are blocked or unavailable, the game continues normally.
