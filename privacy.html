Information processed by the site

This site does not provide user accounts or ask visitors to submit profile information to play the games. The hosting platform and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and requested pages as part of operating and securing the website.

Game data stored in your browser

Rocket Dash may use browser local storage to save your personal-best score on your device. This information is stored locally by your browser and may be cleared through your browser settings. Other games may have their own behavior, which should be checked on the relevant game page.
