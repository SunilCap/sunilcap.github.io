Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how information may be processed when you visit Scotcha. It should be kept accurate as hosting, advertising, analytics, and game features change.

Information processed by the site

This site does not provide user accounts or ask visitors to submit profile information to play the games. Hosting and network providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, request time, and requested pages to operate and secure the website.

Game data stored in your browser

Rocket Dash may use browser local storage to save your personal-best score on your device. This information is stored locally by your browser and may be cleared through browser settings. Other games may behave differently; check the relevant game page.

Advertising and cookies

This site includes Google AdSense code. Ads are not guaranteed to be displayed. If Google ads are served, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, limit, or personalize ads, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

Learn more at Google's advertising technologies and Google's Privacy Policy. Available ad personalization controls are provided through Google My Ad Center.

Third-party services and links

Some pages may link to third-party sites or services. Those sites operate under their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing information.

Children's privacy

This site is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted through a channel we control, contact us and describe the concern without sharing sensitive details publicly.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when site features or legal requirements change. The date above indicates the latest stated revision.
