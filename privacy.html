Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains what information may be processed when you visit Scotcha (scotcha.in) and play its games, including how advertising and third-party services are involved. Scotcha is run by an independent developer.

In short

  • You don't need an account, and you don't give us a name or email address to play.
  • Your scores and settings are saved on your own device, not on our servers.
  • Google ads may be shown, and Google and its partners may use cookies for that.
  • Word Tower checks some words with online dictionary services.

Information processed when you visit

Scotcha is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any website host, GitHub and the network providers involved may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you request and the time of the request, in order to deliver and secure the site. We do not run a separate analytics service on Scotcha at this time. If we add one, this policy will be updated.

Information stored in your browser

Some games use your browser's local storage to remember your progress on that device:

  • Rocket Dash saves your best distance and your bank of collected stars.
  • Stack Up saves your best number of floors.
  • Word Tower saves the tallest tower you have built.
  • Volleyball Duel does not save scores.

This information stays on your device and is not sent to us. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

To let games be installed and played offline, the site also uses a service worker, which keeps copies of the site's pages, scripts and images in your browser's cache. You can remove them by clearing site data or uninstalling the app.

Word Tower dictionary checks

When you submit a word in Word Tower that is not in the game's built-in list, the word is sent to third-party dictionary services (Datamuse and the Free Dictionary API) to check whether it is a real word. These services receive the word you typed and the technical information your browser sends with any web request, such as your IP address. Player names you type into the game stay on your device and are not sent. These services have their own privacy practices.

Advertising and cookies

Scotcha uses Google AdSense and may show ads, including full-screen ads between game rounds and optional rewarded ads, through Google's H5 Games Ads. Ads are not guaranteed to be displayed.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to Scotcha and other sites on the internet. Google may also use technologies such as web beacons and device identifiers to measure and limit ads.

You can manage ad personalization in Google My Ad Center, or opt out of personalized advertising by some third-party vendors at aboutads.info. Learn more about Google's advertising technologies, how Google uses information from sites that use its services, and Google's Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it, for example for visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you may be shown a message asking for your choices about cookies and ad personalization before ads are shown.

Sharing your score

The Share button in Stack Up opens your device's own share menu or copies a link to your clipboard. We do not receive what you share or who you share it with.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and your message. We use them only to respond and to improve the games, and we keep them for as long as needed for that purpose.

Third-party links

Some pages link to other websites. Those sites have their own privacy practices, so please review their policies before sharing information with them.

Children's privacy

Scotcha is a general-audience site and is not designed to collect personal information from children. We do not ask for names, accounts or contact details to play. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

  • Clear your browser's site data to remove saved scores and offline copies.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Use the ad controls linked above to manage personalized ads.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights over your personal information, such as access or deletion. Because we do not keep accounts, the only personal information we normally hold is what you choose to email us. Contact us and we will help.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the site's features or the law change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, email scotchlabs@gmail.com.
