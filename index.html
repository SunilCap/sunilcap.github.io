Volleyball Duel — Cartoon Edition

A fast-paced, cartoon-styled 2-player volleyball game you can play right in your browser — no download required. Play locally with a friend on the same device, challenge a built-in AI opponent, or connect two separate devices over the internet for real-time online matches.

▶ Play Now

About this game

Volleyball Duel started as a personal project to explore building a complete, playable browser game from scratch — physics, animation, multiplayer networking, and mobile-friendly touch controls all included. The game features hand-drawn cartoon-style graphics, a full set of keyboard and on-screen touch controls, smash attacks, and a first-to-10 scoring match format.

It's designed to work well on both desktop and mobile browsers, with a responsive layout that adapts to different screen sizes, plus a fullscreen mode for an immersive play experience.

Features

  • Local 2-player mode — play with a friend on the same device
  • 1-player mode against a built-in AI opponent
  • Online mode — play against a friend on a separate device, anywhere
  • Smash attacks, jump physics, and momentum-based ball control
  • Touch controls optimized for mobile, full keyboard support on desktop
  • Installable as a standalone app on your phone's home screen
