Small games. Quick challenges.

Welcome to an independent collection of browser-game projects. The games here are designed for short sessions, straightforward controls, and play without installing a separate app. Each game page includes controls and tips to help you get started.

This collection is a work in progress; new features and games may be added over time.

How this site works

These are browser-based projects. Use the game page's controls and instructions, and return here to choose another game. Rocket Dash, Stack Up, and Word Tower store your personal best (and, in Rocket Dash, your banked stars) in your browser's local storage when that feature is available; clearing browser data may reset it.

Accessibility and device tips

  • Use an up-to-date browser for the best compatibility.
  • On desktop, Rocket Dash supports Space, Arrow Up, or W for thrust, and Stack Up supports Space or Enter to drop a block.
  • On touch devices, tap the play area to control Rocket Dash and Stack Up.
  • Rocket Dash, Stack Up, and Word Tower have a sound control to mute or enable synthesized sound.
  • Every game has a full-screen option, and you can add Scotcha or any single game to your home screen to play it like an app, including offline once it has been opened.

About the creator and projects

This site is an independent home for small web experiments and playable prototypes. Each project is maintained as time allows, and the guides explain the intended gameplay rather than promising features that may not be available in every version.
