How this site works

These are browser-based projects. Use the game page's controls and instructions, and return here to choose another game. Rocket Dash stores its personal-best score in your browser's local storage when that feature is available; clearing browser data may reset it.

Accessibility and device tips

  • Use an up-to-date browser for the best compatibility.
  • On desktop, Rocket Dash supports Space, Arrow Up, or W for thrust.
  • On touch devices, tap the play area to control the rocket.
  • Use the sound control in Rocket Dash to mute or enable synthesized sound.

About the creator and projects

This site is an independent home for small web experiments and playable prototypes. Each project is maintained as time allows, and the guides explain the intended gameplay rather than promising features that may not be available in every version.
