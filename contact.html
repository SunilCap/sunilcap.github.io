Contact Scotcha

Questions, bug reports, ideas for new games, or feedback are all welcome. The best way to reach us is by email:

scotchlabs@gmail.com

Reporting a bug

The more detail you can include, the faster we can fix it. Please tell us:

  • Which game you were playing.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Your device and browser, for example "iPhone, Safari" or "Windows, Chrome".
  • Whether you were playing in the browser or from the home-screen app.
  • A screenshot, if you can.

Other messages

  • Feedback and ideas: tell us what you enjoyed, what felt too easy or too hard, and what you would like to see next.
  • Privacy requests: see our Privacy Policy, then email us with your question.
  • Anything else: just write to us and we will point you in the right direction.

Before you write

Please do not include passwords or payment details in your message. We read every email, and replies may take a few days. Games are listed on the Games page, and each game has a how-to-play guide that answers common questions.
