Feedback and bug reports

The most useful reports describe what happened, what you expected, which device and browser you used, and the steps needed to reproduce the issue.

How to get in touch

For project feedback, bug reports, or suggestions, use the GitHub Issues page for this website repository:

Open project issues

What to include in a report

  • Which game or page you were using.
  • Your device and browser, including the browser version if known.
  • The steps that led to the issue.
  • A screenshot or error message, if it helps explain the problem.

Please do not post passwords, payment details, or other private information in public issue reports.

Email contact

Please do not email passwords, payment information, or other sensitive personal data.
