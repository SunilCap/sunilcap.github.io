About Scotcha

Scotcha hosts small browser-game projects and experiments. The goal is to make lightweight games that open directly in a browser, with controls that are easy to learn and short gameplay loops that reward practice.

What you can play

Rocket Dash

Rocket Dash is an original one-tap arcade game. Control a rocket moving through a scrolling space course, avoid gate obstacles, collect stars, and try to improve your personal-best distance.

Play Rocket Dash · Read the Rocket Dash guide

Volleyball Duel

Volleyball Duel is a cartoon-style volleyball game. Its available modes and controls are presented in the game and may change as the project develops.

Play Volleyball Duel · Read the Volleyball Duel guide

Word Tower

Word Tower is a word-building game where players build a tower by entering valid words. Follow the instructions shown in the game for the current rules and timer.

Play Word Tower

Updates and feedback

Games may be improved over time based on testing and player feedback. To report a bug or suggest a change, email scotchlabs@gmail.com.
