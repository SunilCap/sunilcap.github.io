About this game collection

This website hosts small browser-game projects and experiments. The goal is to make lightweight games that open directly in a browser, with controls that are easy to learn and short gameplay loops that reward practice.

What you can play

Rocket Dash

Rocket Dash is an original one-tap arcade prototype. The player controls a rocket moving through a scrolling space course, avoids gate obstacles, collects stars, and tries to improve a personal-best distance. The game includes a start screen, pause/resume controls, a sound toggle, and a game-over/restart flow.

Play Rocket Dash · Read the Rocket Dash guide

Volleyball Duel

Volleyball Duel is a separate cartoon-style volleyball project linked from this site. Its available modes and controls are presented in the game itself and may change as the project is developed.
