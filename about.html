About Scotcha

Scotcha is an independent collection of small browser games. The goal is simple: games that open instantly in your browser, with controls you can learn in seconds and short rounds that reward practice. There is nothing to download and no account to create.

What you can play

Rocket Dash

A one-tap arcade game. Tap to thrust a little rocket through a bright, scrolling sky, thread it between gates, collect stars and power-ups, and beat your best distance. Stars you collect can be spent to keep a run going.

Play Rocket Dash · Read the Rocket Dash guide

Stack Up

A one-tap timing game. A block slides across the screen, and you drop it onto a growing tower. Overhanging parts are sliced off, and perfect drops build a combo. The sky changes from day to night as your tower climbs.

Play Stack Up · Read the Stack Up guide

Volleyball Duel

A cartoon-style volleyball game. Play a friend on the same device or take on the computer, with keyboard controls on a computer and on-screen buttons on a phone. First to ten points wins the match.

Play Volleyball Duel · Read the Volleyball Duel guide

Word Tower

A word-chain game for 2 to 8 players. Each word must begin with the last letter of the previous word, and every word becomes a slab in a colorful tower. Beat the timer, and be the last player standing. Play with friends on one device or against computer opponents.

Play Word Tower · Read the Word Tower guide

How the games are made

  • Quick to start. Every game opens from one page and starts in a tap.
  • Works on any screen. The games are built for phones first and also work with a keyboard on a computer.
  • Play like an app. Each game can be added to your home screen to run full screen, and works offline once it has been opened.
  • Light on your data. Scores are saved on your own device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Free to play, supported by ads

Scotcha is free. To help cover the cost of running and improving the games, the site may show ads from Google, including short ads between rounds and optional ads that earn bonus rewards. We try to keep them at natural breaks so they don't interrupt play.

Updates and feedback

Games are improved over time based on testing and player feedback, and new games are added when they are ready. To report a bug or suggest a change, see our Contact page or email scotchlabs@gmail.com.
